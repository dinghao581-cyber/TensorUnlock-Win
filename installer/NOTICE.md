## 4. Upstream disclaimer

- This solution modifies GPU registers (it does not flash the VBIOS or modify driver files), which is an operation the vendor **does not support**.
- Unlock values are volatile: they are lost when power is removed, so unlocking must be rerun at every boot (this solution automates that).
- It may affect warranty, stability and driver compatibility. Use it at your own risk; the author accepts no liability for any loss.
